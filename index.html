ShroudChat

Privacy-focused AI chat · 100% on-device

In the building stage

ShroudChat is a privacy-focused AI chat app that runs entirely on your phone. No servers, no accounts, no tracking — your conversations never leave your device. The AI model runs locally, so replies are instant and work even offline.

⚡

Local on-device AI

A capable language model runs right on your device for instant replies — nothing sent to the cloud.

🔒

No account needed

Open the app and start chatting. No sign-up, no phone number, no email. Your identity stays yours.

✈️

Truly offline

No internet? No problem. Everything runs on-device, so it works on a plane, on a mountain, anywhere.

🛡️

Private by design

Conversations live only on your device. Delete them and they're gone — for good. Nothing is ever uploaded.

🔇

No telemetry

Zero analytics, zero tracking SDKs, zero behavioral profiling. We don't watch what you type — we can't.

🚀

Fast & responsive

Because the model lives on your device, responses are instant — no network round-trips, no waiting on a server.
