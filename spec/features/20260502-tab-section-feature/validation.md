# Validation - Tab Section

## Verification Steps
1. **Visual Match**: Verify the "active" tab has the orange underline.
2. **Tab Alignment**: Ensure tabs are left-aligned and stay within the central container width.
3. **Count Bubbles**: Verify that numeric counts (if added) are styled in small circles/pills.
4. **Responsiveness**: Resize the browser to ensure "Packages" and "Stars" are hidden or moved to the "More" section as planned.
5. **Hover States**: Verify that non-active tabs show a light grey background or underline on hover.
